What is Digital Safety?

Digital safety means protecting our personal information, devices, and accounts while using the internet.

Common Online Threats

  • Phishing Scams: Fake messages or emails trying to trick you into giving away secrets like passwords.
  • Malware & Viruses: Harmful files that can sneak onto your device and ruin your files or steal data.
  • Identity Theft: Strangers using your private info to pretend to be you online.

Safety Tips

  • Keep Passwords Secret: Never share your passwords with friends or type them on shared devices.
  • Think Before Clicking: Avoid clicking strange links or downloads from people you don't know.
  • Use Two-Step Verification: Add an extra lock to your accounts so only you can log in.

Prevention Guide

  • Update Your Devices: Always install software updates promptly to patch safety holes.
  • Secure Your Wi-Fi: Avoid accessing bank or private accounts on free public Wi-Fi networks.
  • Backup Your Data: Keep copies of your important school projects or photos on a flash drive or safe cloud server.
