How to Prevent Malware

1. Install Antivirus Software

Use trusted antivirus software to detect and remove malware.

2. Keep Software Updated

Always update your operating system and applications to fix security vulnerabilities.

3. Avoid Suspicious Links

Do not click unknown links or download files from untrusted websites.

4. Use Strong Passwords

Create strong and unique passwords for your online accounts.

5. Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Add another layer of security to protect your accounts.

6. Avoid Pirated Software

Download programs only from official and trusted sources.

7. Back Up Important Files

Keep copies of important data in cloud storage or external drives.

8. Be Careful with Email Attachments

Do not open suspicious attachments from unknown senders.

9. Use Secure Wi-Fi Networks

Avoid connecting to unknown or unsecured Wi-Fi networks.

10. Scan External Devices

Scan USB drives and other external devices before opening their files.
